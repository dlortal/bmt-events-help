At high-profile Events, efficient Attendee management is crucial. While the current Events system allows Ticket Templates to be assigned based on Ticket Type, this is not flexible enough for groups such as Nominees, Press, and Members, who may require distinct ticket designs regardless of their Ticket Type. To streamline check-in and ensure a smooth entry process, Admins need the ability to assign specific ticket templates to individual Attendees.

This guide outlines the steps to allocate customised ticket designs, helping the Event Organisers implement a clear, visually distinct system that improves crowd flow and enhances the Attendee experience.

## Creating Multiple Ticket Templates

Ticket templates are managed at the Branch level. To create a new template:

1.  Navigate to **Control centre > Branches**.
2.  Find the relevant Branch and select **Actions > Edit**.
3.  Enable **Display ticket design per ticket type** by switching the option to **Yes**.
4.  Scroll down to the **Ticket Templates** section.
5.  Click **Add** to create a new template.
6.  Enter a **Name** for the template.
7.  Upload a **PDF** of the ticket layout.
8.  Define the **position and size** of ticket elements.
9.  Click **Save & Close**.

## Bulk Updating Ticket Designs by Ticket Type

Tickets are automatically assigned based on existing ticket rules, that are set on the [Event Settings.](/help-articles/how-to-create-a-standard-event/) However, Admins can override the default template via the Guest List. To update ticket designs in bulk:

1.  Go to **Events List > Locate your event > Via the action button on the right > Guest List**.
2.  Use the column filters to find the relevant **Ticket Type**.
3.  Click **Change Ticket Design**.
4.  Select the appropriate design and click **OK** - the new template will be applied.
5.  Repeat the process for any additional Ticket Types as needed.
