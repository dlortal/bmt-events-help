The check-in view works on any phone or tablet with a camera.

1. Open the event and choose **Check-in**.
2. Point the camera at the ticket's code. A green tick confirms the guest.
3. If a guest has no ticket, search by name and select **Check in** next to their entry.

Guests who have already been checked in show a time stamp, so duplicate arrivals are easy to spot.
