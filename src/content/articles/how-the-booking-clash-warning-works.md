Whenever a member tries to book tickets for an Event whose date and time conflicts with another booking they might have already processed, the system will alert them with a red warning message that shows up in the Event's page, which says:

**This event conflicts with an existing booking**

If the Booker tries to click on **Book tickets**, they will also be presented with **a pop-up message** asking them to confirm their intention of book, that says:  
  
**Event time conflict  
This event overlaps with one of your existing bookings. You can review your bookings now, or continue with this booking.**  
  
And with 2 clickable buttons saying:

-   **Continue with booking**: Will prompt the Booker to continue their booking process
-   **View existing bookings** Will redirect them to their existing bookings page

When all-day events with multiple sessions are open for bookings, users frequently book into multiple sessions that overlap. This also occurs during periods with overlapping screenings or staggered start times. This warning message's goal is to give more awareness to members, and promote a more effective tickets allocation.

This message can be edited by Admins at will from the **CMS** section through the following steps:

1.  Log into the **Admin Interface**.
2.  Navigate to the desired **Branch** where you wish to apply the change to in the dropdown option in the top left corner.
3.  Go to the **Control centre** tab in the left hand menu and click on **CMS** form the new menu.
4.  In the **Label** column header search for:
    -   This event conflicts with an existing booking
    -   event conflicts popup heading
    -   event conflicts popup body
    -   event conflicts popup button yes
    -   event conflicts popup button no
5.  In order to modify them, Hover with the mouse over **Actions** on the left-hand side of each result, and click on **Edit**.
6.  If you wish to apply the copy change to the entire system and all branches, add the new desired text in the **Global Content** text box. If you wish to only change the text for the specific **Branch** you navigated into, do not touch the text present in the Global Content, but just add/change the one in the \[Branch Name\] Content (I.e. if you are in the London Branch, it will be called **London Content**).
7.  Click on **Save** or **Save & Close** before exiting.
