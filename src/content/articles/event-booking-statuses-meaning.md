On the Events platform, once an Event is open, bookings start to flow in, and depending on the individual Event’s booking requirements and setup, these bookings might appear with different statuses assigned to them, as well as specific colour coding. To view an Event’s bookings list:

1.  Log into the **Admin Interface**
2.  Click on the **Events List** tab in the left hand menu
3.  Locate the Event you want to see the Bookings list for, hover over **Actions** on its right-hand side
4.  Click on **Bookings**, which will prompt you to the booking list page
5.  Another way to reach this page is to click on the Event’s name to open its **Basic Details** page. Once there, at the top menu there is a clickable tab called **Bookings**, clicking on it will also redirect to the bookings list page.
