Events uses your existing BAFTA account, so there is no separate password to remember.

1. Go to the Events sign-in page.
2. Enter the email address on your BAFTA record.
3. Enter your password and select **Sign in**.

## Forgotten your password?

Select **Forgotten password** on the sign-in page and follow the emailed link. The link expires after one hour.
